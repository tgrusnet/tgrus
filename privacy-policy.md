# Privacy Policy for TG Client RU

Last updated: May 3, 2026

## Introduction

TG Client RU is an unofficial client based on the official Telegram open-source client.
All data processing is handled by Telegram's servers in the same manner as the official app.

## Data Handling

This application does not collect, store, or process any user data independently.
All messages, contacts, and account data are processed exclusively by Telegram's infrastructure.

## Third-Party Services

This app uses Telegram's official servers and follows Telegram's privacy practices.
No additional third-party analytics or advertising services are included.
